How the charts behave

Open a layout and use the 1M / 6M / 1Y / 5Y links, or a chart's own thumbnails. The switcher is the relay page's CSS, with no JavaScript. A period link changes both charts. A thumbnail changes one.

The series are illustrative. Throughput is the sum of relay write and read in Gbit/s, with the sum of advertised capacity as the dashed line. Uptime is the mean Running flag. Periods match a relay page: 1 month, 6 months, 1 year, 5 years.

The charts are not drawn inside the Bandwidth Capacity or Relay Uptime cards. Those cards are a third of the ribbon, narrower than layout C, and they are already the tallest cards in the first row.
