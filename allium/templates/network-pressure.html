Network Pressure

Overload is the status: the share of relays whose server descriptor that day includes an overload-general line. It is a level, about a day late, because a relay writes the line into its next descriptor and keeps it for 72 hours. It is pressure, not a cause. Guard confirms a problem that lasts for weeks. HSDir is context. Directory bytes served are the bytes relays sent while answering directory requests. The same 1 month, 6 months, 1 year, and 5 years as a relay page. A thumbnail changes that chart. A period link changes all four.
